About Greenfield High

Our story, mission, and the people who make it happen.

Our Story

Nearly four decades of learning

Founded in 1985, Greenfield High School began with just 150 students and three classrooms. Today we serve over 1,200 students across a full range of academic, artistic, and athletic programs, while holding onto the close-knit spirit we started with.

Mission

What we stand for

Curiosity

We encourage students to ask questions and explore ideas beyond the textbook.

Integrity

We hold ourselves to high standards of honesty and respect, in and out of class.

Community

We believe school is stronger when students, families, and staff work as one team.
