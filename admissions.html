Admissions

Everything you need to apply to Greenfield High.

How to Apply

Four simple steps

Step 1

1. Submit Application

Complete our online application form with student and guardian details.

Step 2

2. Provide Records

Submit transcripts and records from the student's current school.

Step 3

3. Interview

Meet with our admissions team for a short, friendly conversation.

Step 4

4. Decision

Families are notified of admission decisions within two weeks.

Requirements

What you'll need

  • Completed application form
  • Most recent report card or transcript
  • One letter of recommendation from a current teacher
  • Proof of residency
  • $50 non-refundable application fee
Key Dates

Admissions calendar

Milestone Date
Applications open September 1
Priority deadline January 15
Interviews February
Decisions released March 1
Enrollment deadline April 1
